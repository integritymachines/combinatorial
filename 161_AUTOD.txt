AUTOD — Automotive Development
v0.1 | 8 October 2026 | Problem layer

SCOPE
Industry: ##AUTOMOTIVE. Primary Domain: 102 #PROD. Territory: #DEVELOPMENT. Macro Domain anchor: #TRANS.
Split-from reference: 160 AUTOM.
Physical vehicle and component definitions, product-family commonality, prototype configurations and development validation define this Industry Domain. It includes discrete design choices and physical placement contracts before routine realization.
Manufacturing capability and realization of released designs remain AUTOM. Supplier commitments, independent movement, workforce arrangements, commercial offers, chemical formulation and installed digital-resource operation remain with SCM, LOG, HR, MKT, PROC and COMP when those contracts define the decision.

#design | 1##

AUTOD101 Product-Family Platform Selection.
DESCRIPTION Assign vehicle variants to shared or distinct platforms and module definitions under compatibility and performance requirements.
TEMPLATE Product-platform and commonality engineering | Mixed-discrete family optimization; decomposition and multiobjective search; bounded module-count enumeration with continuous inner optimization.
REGIME #design | Commits shared product definitions before individual vehicle configurations.
STRUCTURE #map + #select | Assigns variants to platform or module definitions and selects the retained definitions.
PRIORITY Critical | Commonality commitments constrain the product-family design stack.
RELEVANCE Critical | Better commonality can reduce engineering and manufacturing investment across vehicle families.
HARDNESS High | Shared definitions must balance compatibility, performance and coupled family costs.
DEPENDENCIES ∅ → AUTOD101 → AUTOD102, AUTOD103, AUTOD104, AUTOD105, AUTOD111, AUTOD901.
TAGS #TRANS #PROD ##AUTOMOTIVE #DEVELOPMENT #design #map #select ##product-platform ##commonality ##product-family.
COMMENTS Commercial assortment is a separate contract. Shared motor and battery module designs are variants of this Problem.
