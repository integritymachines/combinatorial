REF — Refining
v0.1 | 9 October 2026 | Problem layer

SCOPE
Macro Domain anchor: #TRANS. Primary Domain: 101 #PROC. Industry: ##REFINING. Territory: #PRODUCTION.
REF covers refinery transformation capability, feed preparation, process-unit production, material-state storage, product blending and process-coupled maintenance. It includes process heat recovery, reactant hydrogen reuse and contaminant-based water integration.
Ownership follows process capability, recipe validity or material-state feasibility. Independent transport, procurement, supply positions, utility production, workforce rosters, construction, extraction and trading remain with their corresponding Domains. Designing refinery production assets remains within this production territory.

REF101 Refinery Process Configuration Design.
DESCRIPTION Selects refining technologies, unit capacities and material connections to deliver a target product slate under feedstock, investment and process limits.
TEMPLATE Refinery flowsheet synthesis and retrofit screening | Superstructure MILP or MINLP with technology-selection logic.
REGIME #design | Commits installed transformation capability before production planning.
STRUCTURE #link + #select | Constructs material-processing connections and selects participating technologies and capacity options.
PRIORITY Critical | Process configuration defines the refinery's feasible transformations.
RELEVANCE Critical | Configuration commits capital and long-lived conversion capability.
HARDNESS High | Technology alternatives, recycles and nonlinear yields require careful model reduction.
DEPENDENCIES ∅ → REF101 → REF102, REF103, REF104, REF105, REF106, REF301.
