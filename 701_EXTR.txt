EXTR Resource Extraction Systems
v0.1 | Problem layer | 2026-10-07

EXTR covers discrete decisions defined by prospecting, extraction, depletion, wells and resource blocks. It spans mining, quarrying, oil and gas, and groundwater extraction. Land rights, standalone transport, process recipes and installed utility operation retain their own Primary Domains.

The sequence follows initial commitments from exploration and resource design to extraction plans, material allocation and well operation. Dependencies are applicable industrial or information handoffs; not every deployment uses every linked Problem. Feedback and joint-design couplings remain in background/701_EXTR_research.txt. Templates describe established model families, not a universal method ranking.

EXTR01 Exploration Prospect Portfolio Selection.
DESCRIPTION Selects resource prospects for exploration under exploration budgets, geological dependence and access limits.
TEMPLATE Risked prospect evaluation and exploration portfolio planning | Binary stochastic or multiobjective portfolio optimization.
