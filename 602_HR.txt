602 HR — Workforce & Organizational Systems
v0.1 | Problem layer | 2026-10-07

HR covers workforce capacity, qualifications, staffing and coordinated human activity. It includes education, sports and organizational calendars. Standalone clinical staff rosters belong to HR; patient-specific care decisions belong to HC. Movement-led operations belong to LOG. Commercial labor-market mechanisms belong to MKT.

Dependencies describe conditional handoffs within a shared operating system. Independent industry branches need not occur together. Integrated models can co-decide connected Problems. Ratings apply to the stated scope. Evidence, alternatives and planning returns remain in background/602_HR_research.txt.
