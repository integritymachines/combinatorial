MKT Market and Commercial Allocation
v0.1 | Problem layer | 2026-10-07
Normative: 000_DOMAIN.txt v0.4. Evidence: background/501_MKT_research.txt v0.1.

MKT covers discrete commercial choices over ranges, offer menus, prices, exposure, bids, traded rights and market assignments. The set spans offer definition, commercial planning, market allocation and live selling or serving. Physical supply and execution remain in their owning Domains. Financial positions and settlement belong to FIN; physical grid operation to ENER; workforce commitments to HR; clinical allocation to HC; physical land and tenant layouts to LAND; installed digital-resource operation to COMP.

Each card requires a material discrete artifact. Continuous prices, flows, forecasts and control multipliers can supply inputs or accompany it. Templates name defensible established architectures, with distinct applicability conditions. They do not assert a universal performance ranking or public reproducibility of private systems.

Dependencies are conditional industrial or information handoffs. The order places supplied policies before their consumers and bidder participation before auction awards. Three returns update a later planning iteration. Integrated models can co-decide connected Problems. Ratings apply to the stated scope. Cases await human approval.
