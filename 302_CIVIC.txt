CIVIC Civic Infrastructure and Construction
v0.1 | Problem layer | 2026-10-07
Normative: 000_DOMAIN.txt. Evidence: background/302_CIVIC_research.txt v0.1.

CIVIC decides the configuration, access, upkeep and construction execution of shared civic assets and services. The set spans capital commitments, physical design, service allocation, public works, live street coordination and recovery.
Ownership follows the decision contract. Pure journeys and vehicle duties belong to LOG; standalone workforce decisions to HR; installed utility balance and operating recovery to ENER; basin and land-use allocation to LAND. Public ownership alone does not establish CIVIC.
Dependencies are conditional decision flow, not a required integrated model. First-pass flow determines sequence; inspection and operational recovery returns remain explicit. Cases require human approval of the Problem set.
