RET — Retail Merchandising
v0.1 | 9 October 2026 | Problem layer

SCOPE
Macro Domain anchor: #EXCH. Primary Domain: 501 #MKT. Industry: ##RETAIL. Territory: #DISTRIBUTION.
Merchandise ranges, commercial bundle menus, category and item display space, posted prices, promotions, targeted offers, curated subscription content and live retail presentation define this territory. It covers physical, digital, omnichannel and subscription retail.
Ownership follows the delivered commercial offer, merchandise presentation or curated content commitment. Stock quantities, sourcing, replenishment and fixed-order fulfillment remain SCM contracts. Physical handling and delivery, workforce schedules, financial positions, manufacturable product definitions and installed digital-resource operation remain with their corresponding Domains.

RET101 Category Space Configuration.
DESCRIPTION Configure category selling locations and discrete fixture-space shares within a supplied store layout under category capacity, presentation and demand-response conditions.
TEMPLATE Macro-space and category-space management | Store-wide space-allocation IP or MINLP with hierarchical product-allocation decomposition.
REGIME #design | Configures category presentation capacity before item ranges and display plans are committed.
STRUCTURE #place + #map | Positions category blocks and assigns discrete fixture-space modules to categories.
PRIORITY High | Category space defines the presentation capacity of major physical retail formats.
RELEVANCE High | Better category footprints and locations can improve store margin and visibility.
HARDNESS High | Category demand, customer traffic and downstream item allocations couple the store layout.
DEPENDENCIES ∅ → RET101 → RET301, RET303.
