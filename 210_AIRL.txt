AIRL — Airline Operations
v0.1 | 8 October 2026 | Problem layer

SCOPE
Macro Domain anchor: #DISP. Primary Domain: 201 #LOG. Industry: ##AIRLINE. Territory: #PRODUCTION.
Airline transport capability, flight programs, aircraft circulation, physical journey and load assignments, airline-controlled ground movement and disruption recovery define this territory. It covers passenger, combination and cargo carriers.
Ownership follows the delivered airline movement program. Standalone crew systems, commercial offers and access rights, supply availability, financial claims, detailed repair-work calendars and shared airport service or infrastructure contracts remain with their corresponding Domains.
Gate, ground-service and cargo-terminal Problems apply when the carrier controls the operating movement contract.
