SEMIM — Semiconductor Manufacturing
v0.1 | 10 October 2026 | Case layer

SCOPE
Macro Domain anchor: #TRANS. Primary Domain: 102 #PROD. Industry: ##SEMICONDUCTOR. Territory: #PRODUCTION.
Fabrication, assembly, manufacturing inspection and test, and physical device repair of released semiconductor designs define this territory. Manufacturing capability configuration and reticle preparation remain production. Chip, circuit and package architecture belong to development.
Fabrication feasibility determines ownership. Standalone process chemistry, supply commitments, carrier movement, workforce arrangements, utility operation, commercial rights and digital-resource operation remain with PROC, SCM, LOG, HR, ENER, MKT and COMP when those contracts define the decision.

SEMIM701c Fab-Wide Reentrant Scheduling Benchmark.
CASE Assign and sequence released wafer-lot operations across an interacting fab under reentry, qualification, batching, setup, time-link, maintenance and WIP-flow constraints.
STAGE Researched INDUSTRY | Hierarchical fab-wide plus local scheduling is deployed and current; public testbeds do not reproduce the full industrial state and constraint system.
SOTA Rolling hierarchical scheduling combines live MES/factory state, fab-wide look-ahead and local toolset optimization, with dispatch or rule logic retained as an execution or fallback layer.
CONTEXT Front-end wafer fabrication | Thousands of lots, hundreds or more productive tools, long reentrant routes, sparse qualifications, batching, setups, reticles or secondary resources, time links, maintenance, failures and priority classes.
GAP Real deployments show gains from global/hierarchical optimization, but common open MiniFab, MIMAC and SMT2020-style testbeds omit material real-fab complexity and current industrial datasets remain proprietary | Proposed uplift direction: test v10 global/local decomposition and coordination on a purpose-built rolling fab benchmark against strong dispatch, local CP/MIP, hierarchical scheduling and simulation/AI baselines.
ATTEMPTS Current practice spans expert/configurable dispatch rules, local CP/MIP and heuristic schedulers, hierarchical fab-wide look-ahead, simulation-enabled optimization and emerging AI/RL policies; recent industrial evidence shows local optimization can improve locally while giving inconsistent fab-wide results without global information.
TAGS ##hierarchical-scheduling ##global-local ##reentrant ##rolling-horizon ##benchmark-gap.
COMMENTS Local batch, lithography and cluster-tool calendars remain SEMIM702-704 when separately contracted; live lot release and immediate tool dispatch remain SEMIM801-802.

SEMIM701dsim Fab-Wide Reentrant Scheduling Candidate.
CASE Coordinate wafer-lot schedules across multiple work centers with simulation-based fab state, rolling schedule generation and real-time dispatch between schedule updates.
STAGE Researched CANDIDATE | Public evidence supports a mature deployed scheduling, dispatch and simulation stack; full-fab objective coverage and proprietary decision logic remain undisclosed.
STATE Semiconductor-specific digital-twin suite with automated model calibration, WIP forecasting, multi-work-center schedule synchronization, real-time dispatch and simulation-based evaluation of scheduling or AI policies.
CONTEXT Semiconductor software and engineering vendor | Deployed across multiple 6-, 8- and 12-inch fabs; integrated scheduling, dispatch, WIP-flow and AI-evaluation infrastructure.
GAP Public evidence reports local and critical-equipment gains but does not show whether the incumbent scheduler optimizes one explicit fab-wide global objective or how local gains propagate across interacting bottlenecks | Proposed uplift direction: add and benchmark a v10 global/local coordination layer that prices downstream bottleneck, WIP-wave and due-date consequences before local schedule changes are committed.
ATTEMPTS Existing methods include multi-objective scheduling/dispatching, smart heuristics with parallel computing, automated digital-twin calibration, WIP forecasting, preventive-maintenance synchronization and simulation-based AI/scheduler evaluation.
TAGS ##simulation-enabled ##multi-work-center ##scheduler-dispatcher ##ai-evaluation ##global-local.
COMMENTS Reported customer gains are company evidence; the scheduler objective, decomposition and benchmark data are not public.
