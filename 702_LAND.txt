LAND Land Use and Renewable Resource Systems
v0.1 | Problem layer | 2026-10-08

LAND covers discrete land, geospatial-asset, basin and renewable-resource commitments. It includes physical property allocation, forestry, agriculture, fisheries, conservation and ecological recovery.

The sequence follows initial commitments from spatial configuration and resource access to use calendars, execution and recovery. Dependencies are applicable industrial or information handoffs; no deployment must use every linked Problem. Feedback and conditional directions remain in background/702_LAND_research.txt. Templates are established model families, not a universal method ranking.
