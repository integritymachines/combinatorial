INTEL Defense, Intelligence and Security
v0.1 | Problem layer | 2026-10-07
Normative: 000_DOMAIN.txt. Evidence: background/402_INTEL_research.txt v0.1.

INTEL decides protective, adversarial and strategic-response commitments. The set spans defense capability, security-system design, intelligence collection, protective operations and cyber defense.
Ownership follows the decision contract. Military context alone does not move generic sensing, transport, provisioning or staffing into INTEL. Those decisions remain COMP, LOG, SCM or HR. Routine utility and civic-service operations remain ENER and CIVIC.
Dependencies are conditional decision flow. Initial plans precede execution and explicit feedback returns. Templates identify supported reference approaches; a research method is not automatically deployed practice. Cases require human approval of this Problem set.
