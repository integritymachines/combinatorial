Normative Domain: 301 #ENER.

Energy and utility optimization configures or operates physical supply. This set covers power, gas, heat, cooling and installed water systems. Each Problem requires a genuine discrete asset, mode, connection, assignment or action decision. Ownership follows utility-service feasibility; basin rights, extraction, process transformation, transport duties and commercial mechanisms remain interfaces.
Dependencies record deployment handoffs when the named Problems occur together. Existing assets and commitments may be supplied directly. Joint optimization and feedback can replace the displayed staged sequence.
