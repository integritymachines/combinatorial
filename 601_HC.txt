HC Healthcare and Human-Critical Operational Systems
v0.1 | Problem layer | 2026-10-07

HC covers discrete decisions whose feasibility or service materially depends on clinical conditions, patients or direct care. It spans clinical service design, care scheduling, patient-resource allocation, medical response and clinically constrained transplantation, transfusion and public health. Generic workforce, supply, transport and construction decisions retain their own Primary Domains.

The sequence follows first commitments from service design and capacity to patient allocation, care execution and repair. Dependencies denote applicable industrial or information inputs; not every deployment uses every linked Problem. Research and evidence: background/601_HC_research.txt. Clinical eligibility, prescriptions and priority policies are supplied unless a card explicitly chooses among approved alternatives.
