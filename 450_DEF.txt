DEF — Defense
v0.1 | 9 October 2026 | Problem layer

SCOPE
Macro Domain anchor: #INFO. Primary Domain: 402 #INTEL. Industry: ##DEFENSE. Territory: #PRODUCTION.
DEF covers the configuration and operation of defense services whose adversarial, protective, denial or strategic-response conditions define the decision. It includes operational force configuration, readiness, military collection, mission commitments, protective security and cyber defense.
Ownership follows the delivered defense service commitment. Military context alone does not transfer ordinary transport, inventory, workforce, manufacturing, utility or digital-resource decisions into DEF. New weapon and platform development, acquisition-program portfolios, procurement transactions and financial administration remain outside this operational service territory.
