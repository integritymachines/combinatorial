IDENTITY AND TERRITORY
Macro Domain anchor: #TRANS. Primary Domain: 102 #PROD. Industry: ##SEMICONDUCTOR. Territory: #DEVELOPMENT.
Code 171 and mnemonic SEMID are fixed user inputs. Production counterpart and split-from reference: 170 SEMIM. The new member does not recode or narrow SEMIM's published production meaning.

Normative basis: 000_DOMAIN.txt, read-only v0.5; background/000_DOMAIN_research.txt; workflow/0_workflow.txt through workflow/5_output.txt. User requires v0.1 for both artifacts despite the workflow's normal controlled-stage suffix. Stage findings and disposition belong in version-control history. This document preserves research, not a control appendix.

The decision territory includes ASIC and SoC architecture, custom analog/mixed-signal circuits, cell-library development, physical chip implementation, heterogeneous integration and package definition, design-for-test, physical fault-test content and first-silicon product validation. A candidate must have a discrete choice that changes or validates a new semiconductor product definition. Being solved by software does not make its Domain COMP.

Territory split evidence:
Artifact: SEMID delivers a new hardware/netlist/layout/package/test definition or validation commitment; SEMIM delivers manufacturing capability, physical realization and production device dispositions.
Practice: product architecture, logic/physical design, analog layout, DFT and silicon bring-up have a different recurring inventory from fab loading, reentrant scheduling, assembly and tester calendars.
Domain residual: both remain PROD because fabricated product/component feasibility defines their contracts.
Not a variant: the split changes committed substrates and downstream artifacts, not only horizon, department or solver method.
These are ontology applications to [ARCH], [ABC], [ALIGN], [DFT], [LAB] and the published SEMIM scope; sources do not themselves state this project's ontology.

OWNERSHIP AND INTERFACES
PROD owns hardware-definition and physical product-validation decisions. COMP owns installed computation/communication operation, EDA/HPC server workloads, software-only regressions and standalone digital verification contracts. A new physical NoC circuit is included; live packets on that NoC are not.
SEMIM owns released-design realization, production tool capability, reticle image packing/preparation, routine tester calendars and defect-driven physical device repair. Timing inside a designed datapath or embedded test protocol is not a manufacturing calendar.
SCM owns procurement, supply availability and die inventory-to-order commitments. Selecting a physical chiplet type in a new architecture does not commit its supplier quantities.
HR owns independent engineering labor, development-project staffing and generic project calendars; LOG owns independent prototype/device journeys; ENER owns installed utility output; MKT owns product offers/assortment; FIN owns funding/financial positions. These boundaries apply after testing the actual promised contract.

DISCOVERY AND EVIDENCE METHOD
Discovery started from normative domain interfaces, the 170 SEMIM exclusions and the legacy VLSI.md inventory. VLSI.md is a non-normative seed: no old ID, tag, stage or unsupported hardness claim is inherited. Existing 102 PROD and 161 AUTOD were read for nearby product-development and physical-validation boundaries.
Search axes: architecture/integration; behavioral and Boolean synthesis; pipeline and library implementation; partitioning and NoC circuits; power domains; standard-cell and analog layout; chip/package geometry and pins; sizing/clock/power closure; global/detailed/package conductors; DFM finishing; scan/BIST/ATPG/test access; silicon validation; frozen-design ECO.
Search families used included OpenROAD module documentation, Yosys/ABC mapping and synthesis, high-level scheduling/binding literature, chiplet cost/floorplan research, analog topology/layout synthesis, SAT/SMT cell generation, voltage-island design, DFT/ATPG industrial tools, physical testpoint research, TAM/MBIST scheduling, post-silicon adaptive selection and ECO rectification.
Primary evidence consists of official implementation documentation, original research or author-institution records. Abstract-only evidence supports decision identity and method family, not unrestricted algorithm claims, reproducibility or SOTA rankings. Retrieved web dates can refer to crawl dates; publication years/version dates were taken from primary metadata where available.
No benchmark cases, measured uplift or reproduced runtime are asserted at this layer.

Evidence vs assessment:
Each source entry states what it establishes. Candidate ownership, Regime, Structure and ratings are analyst assessments under 000_DOMAIN.txt, not claims quoted from a paper.
Priority reflects centrality across SEMID's inventory; not all Problems appear in every chip program. Relevance reflects the engineering consequences of improving the commitment. Hardness reflects practical coupling and present multistage/custom methods, not a complexity label.
Most formal classes are deliberately omitted: a broad industrial family cannot inherit a theorem for a restricted graph model. Classical retiming is a verified polynomial exception. The legacy claims that all retiming or all assignment is NP-hard are not accepted.
An official tool establishes a credible implementation basis. A vendor adjective does not establish comparative optimality. Where there is no dominant architecture, the Template preserves a defensible established family and material near-equals.

Dependency interpretation and cycles:
Architecture feeds hardware synthesis, circuit partitioning and test architecture. A supplied behavioral design becomes an equivalent logic network, then register boundaries and a library implementation. Reusable cell definitions feed library implementation and subsequent discrete sizing.
Circuit partitioning and NoC architecture feed chip/package geometry. Analog topology feeds custom layout; custom block geometry joins the floorplan. Power domains constrain physical regions and power conductors. Test points and scan connections change the netlist before final placement.
Floorplans and I/O interfaces feed physical placement and package routing. Placement feeds electrical optimization, clocks and power networks, then global route guides and detailed conductors. Completed conductors permit fill and physically informed fault-pattern generation. DFT and patterns feed test-access/session protocols; finalized product/test definitions feed silicon validation.
ECO consumes validation defects or layout/signoff failures and returns revised product definitions to affected synthesis, geometry, routing and tests. This is a real cross-Regime feedback cycle and must remain visible.
Within initial design closure, placement ↔ sizing, placement ↔ scan ordering, floorplan ↔ partitioning, pin assignment ↔ package routing and placement/PDN ↔ test grouping are iterative interfaces. The graph records the principal initial handoff once; a changed frozen commitment is routed through ECO. No claim is made that every design flow is a unique acyclic chronological sequence.
Compiler must group by normative Regime first and then topologically order the initial design-handoff graph. Validation plans remain #plan even though they consume a designed product. ECO remains #adapt even though it returns information to earlier design Problems.

MERGES, EXCLUSIONS AND OPEN EVIDENCE GAPS
Global and detailed standard-cell placement: MERGE into PLACE. Both construct the same completed legal cell-position artifact. Keep continuous optimization and legalization in the Template.
Chip global and detailed routing: KEEP DISTINCT. Coarse capacity/layer guides and final rule-compliant conductor geometry are independently controlled artifacts.
Circuit hierarchy and physical die partitioning: MERGE into PART with retained cost/technology variants. Chiplet partitioning does not become a new Structure or Territory.
Clock sink clustering, topology search and buffer insertion: MERGE into CLOCK. Different tree/mesh methods are variants of clock distribution.
NoC topology generation vs global conductor routing: KEEP DISTINCT. Functional router/link architecture differs from the physical paths that realize specified nets.
Analog topology, standard-cell synthesis and analog/custom layout: KEEP DISTINCT. They deliver an electrical topology, a reusable legal library cell, and an electrically constrained custom-block layout.
DFT architecture, test-point membership, scan stitching, physical test patterns and embedded protocols: KEEP DISTINCT at their supplied-architecture/output boundaries. Compression methods and fault models remain variants.
MBIST grouping and resource/power-constrained sessions: MERGE into TAM. Static embedded protocol definition is distinct from a calendar assigning manufactured products to external testers.
ATPG generation and compaction: MERGE into ATPG. Generation/value assignment and retained-pattern choice jointly define usable fault-test content; solver SAT/ML encoding is not a separate Problem.
Physical timing/voltage/interface validation test selection: ADMIT VALIDATE. Software-only regression selection or generic assertions do not enter solely because the target is a chip.
Reticle image packing, mask-set commitments and exposure/dicing plans: EXCLUDE to SEMIM, already scoped there.
Multiple-patterning assignment/stitches on a fixed released layout and OPC/assist-feature preparation: EXCLUDE to SEMIM when the deliverable is fabrication mask data. MPLD [MPLD] is materially combinatorial. If it changes a still-developing product layout, route the design variant to physical layout/routing rather than creating a new SEMID Problem.
Defect-map redundancy allocation and known-good physical die matching: EXCLUDE to SEMIM; already SEMIM504 and SEMIM503. Designing redundancy capacity is ARCH; designing BIST/repair hardware is DFT.
Standalone wafer/die supply procurement and customer-order allocation: EXCLUDE to SCM. Hardware type selection is an architecture choice until it commits supply quantities.
Standalone engineering staffing/project calendars: EXCLUDE to HR. A hardware clock schedule or test-session protocol is not a human project calendar.
Independent prototype device movement: EXCLUDE to LOG.
EDA server placement/compute scheduling, installed NoC traffic and FPGA digital-resource operation: EXCLUDE to COMP. A new FPGA fabric architecture is a semiconductor product-definition variant; mapping a workload to an installed FPGA is not automatically SEMID.
Commercial semiconductor assortment/offer selection: EXCLUDE to MKT. A technical compatible product architecture is not an offer portfolio.
Pure continuous circuit simulation, fixed-topology device sizing, extraction, timing analysis and thermal analysis: not standalone ICO Problems. They supply evaluation or feasibility information to admitted discrete choices.
Analog transistor/device topology beyond circuit-level block choices, package thermal hardware selection, silicon validation lab equipment calendars and process-development experiment design: discovery leads retained for further evidence. Do not admit a separate card without a distinct contract and established discrete Template. Package thermal constraints refine floorplanning; equipment calendars alone do not establish a product-development contract.

COVERAGE AND UNCERTAINTY
Independent omission searches beyond the legacy list found analog topology search, transistor-level standard-cell synthesis, physical test-point design, MBIST grouping, voltage-domain assignments and package RDL routing. They were assessed explicitly rather than silently folded into generic placement.
Follow-up discoveries were mainly implementation phases already retained, alternative methods, fault/process variants, or production/COMP contracts. This supports a broad v0.1 inventory of recurring ICO commitments, not a proof that all possible industry decisions are exhausted.
Underrepresented evidence: proprietary analog/RF and memory design optimization, enterprise silicon-validation instance data, foundry-specific DFM tradeoffs, and current comparative rankings across commercial EDA products. These limits prevent universal SOTA or completeness claims, not the admission of supported recurring Problems.
No #provision, #reserve, live #dispatch or ongoing #operate commitment is required by the admitted product-definition contracts. Do not force Problems into empty bands for visual coverage. Clock-step and protocol ordering do not imply the #sequence commitment stage.
No ontology identity defect was found requiring a code, mnemonic, Domain or Territory change. No canonical ontology file is modified.
