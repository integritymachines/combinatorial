TERRITORY AND OWNERSHIP

AUTOD delivers a new or revised physical vehicle/component definition and the prototype/validation commitments needed to establish that definition before routine realization. Commercial requirements, operating profiles and available technologies are inputs. Product feasibility, physical interfaces, material/geometry compatibility and validation validity define the admitted decisions.
AUTOM delivers installed manufacturing capability, production programs and the realization of released definitions. A factory capacity design is #design within #PRODUCTION; it is not AUTOD simply because it is called development. Conversely, a prototype validation calendar is #plan within #DEVELOPMENT; not every AUTOD Problem has Primary #design.
Both territories retain #PROD after the Primary Domain test. Their artifacts differ (product definition and development validation versus manufacturing capability/output); their professional inventories differ (platform, physical design and validation engineering versus industrialization and factory control); neither territory is a horizon, department or solver variant. This supports the supplied residual split. AUTOM's existing SCOPE already excludes product-platform and family-architecture work. This run adds AUTOD without recoding, renaming or narrowing AUTOM.
Coverage includes passenger cars, commercial vehicles and their supplied components; combustion, hybrid and electric architectures; body, chassis, powertrain, batteries, physical E/E integration and development testing. Specializations are variants unless they change the decision contract. Motorcycle and off-highway applications can share Templates, but no separate industry identity is asserted here.
Physical E/E package and harness design remain #PROD where a manufacturable vehicle architecture defines feasibility. Software task scheduling, message scheduling, compute-resource placement and standalone observation coverage belong to #COMP. A sensor's physical installation can be a packaging variant; an observation-system design whose promise is sensing coverage must first pass the #COMP test.
Vehicle propulsion architecture remains product development even when drive-cycle energy consumption is an objective. Installed utility operation is #ENER. Battery chemical formulation is #PROC. Catalog component definition is AUTOD; supplier and purchase quantities are #SCM. Physical test-specimen integrity and required product validation define AUTOD test scheduling; a generic staffing roster is #HR and an independent test-vehicle journey is #LOG.
Physical variant definitions and commonality commitments are AUTOD. Saleable assortment, prices and commercial launch opportunities are #MKT. Rights, finance and approval routing are administration contracts. Continuous-only shape, sizing, calibration or CFD optimization has no independent ICO admission.

RESEARCH METHOD AND EVIDENCE LIMITS

Search date: 8 October 2026. Discovery covered platform/commonality, modularization, physical system topology, catalog choices, structural and material design, joining, component packaging, harness/duct routing, tolerances, prototype configuration, validation coverage, test scheduling, design changes and test recovery. Additional searches tested assembly/disassembly, battery architecture, E/E design, sensor placement, development-project planning and production launch boundaries.
Primary publisher pages, author/institution repositories and official industrial product documentation were preferred. Several Elsevier and Taylor & Francis pages refused full-text retrieval; their indexed primary-publisher abstracts supplied only the claims identified below. A blocked full-text page is not evidence that the entire paper or industrial data were inspected. No licensed full-text or benchmark accessibility claim follows from abstract availability.
Evidence labels: DIRECT = a primary source treats the relevant automotive decision; TRANSFER = a method from a closely corresponding product-engineering contract; INFERENCE = ontology ownership, candidate grouping, dependency or qualitative rating derived here. Published optimization is not automatically deployed industrial practice. No universal current SOTA winner is claimed. Method families are retained when assumptions differ.
Ratings are comparative judgments under 000_DOMAIN.txt, not reported numerical survey estimates. Priority reflects recurrence within the supplied territory; Relevance reflects cost, performance, reliability and development commitments; Hardness reflects coupled decisions, nonlinear simulation, compatibility and scale. Formal complexity is omitted unless a matching restriction is evidenced. Solver use on a particular case does not establish full-contract complexity or universal industrial difficulty.

CANDIDATE INVENTORY

Candidate keys are research labels, not permanent Problem IDs. Admission, Regime, Structure, ratings and dependencies below are research conclusions to be checked; C derives the final sequence.

FAMILY — Product-family platform and commonality selection
Decision: determine which product definitions share platforms/modules and which retain distinct parts, under given family requirements and acceptable performance differences. Delivered artifact: commonality commitments and platform-to-variant assignments. Aliases: product-platform selection, family architecture, commonization, generalized commonality. Variants: scalable versus modular families, multi-brand families, shared powertrain modules, carry-over parts.
DIRECT evidence: S01 and S05. IE: platform engineering and performance-loss/commonality analysis. OR: mixed-discrete family optimization; decomposition, multiobjective search and bounded enumeration with continuous inner optimization when the model supports it. S05's limited multiplicity enumeration is a near-equal specialized Template, not a universal optimum guarantee.
Primary #design. Primary #map with Secondary #select: map variant requirements to shared or unique platform/module definitions and select retained definitions. The commonality matrix is chosen; drive cycles and requirements are supplied. No #link merely because an assignment is written as a graph.
Distinct from MODULE, which partitions one product's functions; CONFIG, which chooses a particular bill of components; commercial assortment, which chooses offered opportunities. Family volume/cost inputs do not convert this into #MKT or #SCM. Incoming automotive platform/commonality marketplace candidate is within this scope.
Ratings INFERENCE: Priority Critical; Relevance Critical; Hardness High. Commonality constrains many products and amortizes engineering/investment, but compatibility and performance tradeoffs need tailored models. No universal family-size or complexity claim.
Feeds MODULE, ARCH and CONFIG. Iterative module commonality feedback can revise FAMILY. Dependency directions are engineering-flow inference grounded in the definitions and S05's nested architecture; not mathematical reductions.

MODULE — Product modularization
Decision: partition vehicle functions/components into physical modules given interaction requirements, allowed interfaces and integration limits. Artifact: component/function-to-module grouping. Aliases: modular architecture, DSM clustering, function integration. Variants: physical E/E zones, body modules, supplier-engineered assemblies. Functional software partitioning alone is not admitted.
DIRECT S02; supporting integrated product-structure boundary S23. IE: function/interface analysis and design-structure matrices. OR: constrained clustering or partition optimization; local/evolutionary grouping. No supplied interface is claimed to be constructed. If interface architecture itself changes, ARCH is also present.
Primary #design; Primary #map. Each supplied function/component gets a module role. Given interaction weights guide grouping; they do not earn #link. Industrial classification remains #PROD because the output defines physical modules of the vehicle.
FAMILY defines allowed commonality; MODULE defines product granularity; ARCH connects modules; CONFIG fills module roles. Grouping manufacturing machines and parts into cells is AUTOM, not this contract. Product restructuring for assembly/disassembly is retained as an integrated MODULE/JOIN/TOL variant; no independent route-planning Problem is added on lifecycle terminology alone.
Ratings INFERENCE: Priority High; Relevance High; Hardness High. Repeated in modular product programs; interface tradeoffs and permitted module boundaries make grouping difficult. Not every vehicle project performs fresh partition optimization.
FAMILY → MODULE → ARCH, CONFIG. Physical architecture can reveal grouping changes; that iteration is not a second independently numbered modularization Problem.

ARCH — Physical vehicle system architecture synthesis
Decision: choose participating physical subsystems and their interconnections to realize required modes/functions. Artifact: physical system topology, not its operating trajectory. Aliases: powertrain topology synthesis, system configuration synthesis. Variants: combustion/hybrid/electric powertrains, thermal circuits, battery-module electrical architecture and physical E/E topology where product buildability defines the contract.
DIRECT S03 for drivetrain topology; S22 supports physical E/E relevance. Extension to other physical systems is INFERENCE of the same component/interconnection contract; their specific laws require later Case evidence. IE: system synthesis, interface compatibility and physical feasibility screening. OR: graph-based topology enumeration/search with simulation or nested performance optimization. Retain admissibility screening alongside search; performance-only ranking of arbitrary graphs is insufficient.
Primary #design; Primary #link with Secondary #select: construct physical connections and retain participating subsystem alternatives. Supplied requirements/ports define feasibility. Mechanical/energy conservation constrains the artifact; it is not another Structure. Continuous sizes and simulated control are evaluation or coupled refinement, not separate ICO decisions by themselves.
Distinct from LOADPATH (load-bearing structural skeleton), JOIN (panel attachment sites), ROUTE (spatial realization of supplied endpoint connections) and installed software/communication operation. Battery multiplicity with unchanged topology belongs CONFIG/FAMILY; spatial battery packing belongs PACK.
Ratings INFERENCE: Priority High; Relevance Critical; Hardness High. Architecture can lock performance, cost and feasible physical layouts; dynamic evaluation and invalid topology elimination require specialized search.
FAMILY, MODULE → ARCH → CONFIG, PACK, ROUTE, VALIDATE. Product-change selection may reopen ARCH. Bidirectional ARCH/CONFIG refinement is recognized; the forward definitional order is used unless the case supplies a fixed topology.

CONFIG — Catalog component and module configuration
Decision: assign compatible finite component alternatives and integer module counts to required roles in a specified physical architecture. Artifact: discrete component definition/BOM. Aliases: component selection, catalog sizing, option configuration. Variants: motors, storage modules, suspension components, gears and compatible mechanical options. Evidence is strongest for electrified powertrain components; extensions are contract-level inference, not separate claimed deployments.
DIRECT S04; S05 supplies integer multiplicities. IE: component matching and catalog-based performance/cost tradeoff analysis. OR: simulation-assisted discrete search; mixed-integer formulation when component/performance models permit it. Genetic search has direct automotive evidence; exhaustive/convex-inner comparison is a material near-equal for small module-count sets.
Primary #design; Primary #map. Assign chosen catalog definitions and multiplicities to fixed functional roles. This core contract requires no additional #select: identifying the alternatives assigned to roles already captures configuration. Optional component admission that changes the architecture belongs ARCH or a coupled variant.
Distinct from FAMILY's across-model commonality and ARCH's connectivity. A valid supplier-backed component can be an input; choosing suppliers/quantities is #SCM. Dealer option matching and advertised assortment are #MKT. Continuous-only component dimensions are excluded from independent ICO admission.
Ratings INFERENCE: Priority Critical; Relevance High; Hardness High. Recurs wherever finite components define a new vehicle; coupled performance and compatibility evaluation require tailored search. Linear catalog assignment without coupled physics is easier and must not inherit this Hardness by name alone.
FAMILY, MODULE, ARCH → CONFIG → PACK, TOL, PROTOTYPE, VALIDATE. CONFIG and ARCH iterate in integrated product design.

LOADPATH — Discrete structural topology design
Decision: retain load-bearing candidate members and choose their structural connections under a prescribed envelope and load cases. Artifact: connected structural skeleton. Aliases: frame topology, ground-structure design, member topology. Variants: body/chassis frames, battery enclosure supporting structures, crash load paths where candidate structural alternatives are discrete.
DIRECT S06. IE: structural concept engineering with finite-element feasibility evaluation. OR: discrete topology search with structural analysis; multiobjective evolutionary optimization. This is not a claim that every density-based topology method is combinatorial or that a finite-element mesh alone constitutes ICO.
Primary #design; Primary #link with Secondary #select: build load-bearing relations and retain members. Supplied load cases, mesh and material laws are not chosen Structures. Member connections define the structural skeleton rather than mere graph encoding.
Different from MATERIAL's properties on fixed members and JOIN's attachment pattern on specified mating components. Integrated structural topology/property optimization retains both constituent decisions. Continuous shape optimization and numerical mesh refinement alone are excluded.
Ratings INFERENCE: Priority High; Relevance High; Hardness High. Relevant in structural concept work; coupled stress, stiffness, modal and manufacturing evaluations constrain many topology alternatives.
FAMILY → LOADPATH → MATERIAL, PACK, JOIN, VALIDATE. MATERIAL feedback can invalidate member choices; that cycle is retained as an integrated-design relation.

MATERIAL — Discrete material and section assignment
Decision: assign material grades and finite section/gauge alternatives to specified vehicle structural parts under mass, cost, structural and manufacturing requirements. Artifact: part property schedule; topology can remain fixed. Aliases: multi-material selection, discrete gauge optimization, catalog section sizing. Variants: steel/aluminum/composite alternatives, panel gauges and section catalog choices.
DIRECT S06. IE: material/section tradeoff engineering with structural response evaluation. OR: mixed-discrete multiobjective search, including simulation-assisted evolutionary optimization. Continuous gauges may be inner variables only; their presence does not remove the discrete material/catalog commitment.
Primary #design; Primary #map: assign each part an admissible property class. No #link for a supplied structural mesh. Optional part presence belongs LOADPATH or an integrated variant. Procurement volume and supplier selection remain #SCM; bulk alloy/blend chemistry remains #PROC.
Distinct from CONFIG's functional subsystem options: MATERIAL fixes structural property definitions for already specified parts. The two are integrated only when a case contains both catalogs. This distinction reflects structural/material engineering versus component-system configuration, not separate material types.
Ratings INFERENCE: Priority High; Relevance High; Hardness High. Recurs in lightweight structure programs; nonlinear load cases and manufacture-compatible alternatives constrain choices. No independent formal complexity classification is asserted.
LOADPATH → MATERIAL → JOIN, TOL, VALIDATE. Structural/material feedback is possible and not suppressed by final numbering.

PACK — Physical component packaging
Decision: position and orient specified vehicle components in a permitted physical envelope under nonoverlap, access, mounting and interaction limits. Artifact: component layout. Aliases: vehicle packaging, underhood layout, dashboard layout, component packing. Variants: powertrain, batteries, physical electronic devices, occupants/controls when finite location choices define the design contract.
DIRECT S07; automotive author dissertation evidence can support future extended layouts but is not needed for initial admission. IE: package engineering and geometric clearance/accessibility analysis. OR: constrained geometric layout search with simulation or surrogate evaluation. Published staged packaging/routing is retained as an integrated alternative rather than made a third Problem.
Primary #design; Primary #place. Positions/orientations are chosen; component specifications, keep-out geometry and required interfaces are supplied. A component has a position in structured physical space even if coordinates are continuous; the placement/packing contract includes combinatorial spatial relationships. No claim that arbitrary smooth coordinate optimization is always ICO.
Distinct from ROUTE, which realizes required connections after endpoint locations are supplied. Sensor geometry can be a packaging restriction; dedicated sensing coverage optimization is assessed under #COMP. Stock nesting and production-fixture layout remain AUTOM because they commit manufacturing resources.
Ratings INFERENCE: Priority Critical; Relevance High; Hardness High. Physical fit and access constrain vehicle integration; geometry, orientation and routing coupling make industrial search discontinuous.
ARCH, CONFIG, LOADPATH → PACK → ROUTE, JOIN, TOL, VALIDATE. PACK and ROUTE can iterate; a staged contract uses this forward order.

ROUTE — Harness and duct route design
Decision: choose physical connection paths through a supplied vehicle routing environment for specified endpoint demands, with permissible sharing and exclusion limits. Artifact: routed harness/duct paths. Aliases: cable-harness routing, wire route design, duct layout. Variants: electrical harnesses, fluid pipes and air ducts, subject to appropriate physical rules.
DIRECT S07 and S08. IE: physical route/bundle engineering with keep-out and connection checks. OR: shared-path graph optimization, routing heuristics and mixed-integer network models; A* and geometric refinement for staged route design. The model choice depends on sharing and physical rules. No universal steering rule or all-physics routing SOTA is asserted.
Primary #design; Primary #link. Chosen route segments build connection paths on a supplied graph. Continuous centerline refinement can add #place in a coupled geometric variant; it is not required by the core finite routing contract. The supplied graph does not itself earn #link; the chosen paths do.
Distinct from ARCH's choice of which subsystems connect and PACK's endpoint placement. #LOG movement routing transports entities; this product route defines persistent physical paths. #COMP message routes use installed resources. S08 does not fully optimize bend radii and clip positions; manufacturability validation remains an engineering requirement, not an already solved claim.
Ratings INFERENCE: Priority High; Relevance High; Hardness High. Harness/duct complexity and shared paths recur; coupled route selection and geometric restrictions need tailored search. Independent single-path restrictions may be standard shortest paths; they do not support Low Hardness for the complete industrial contract.
ARCH, PACK → ROUTE → VALIDATE. Routing feedback may revise PACK. No new physical #ENER utility-network identity is introduced.

JOIN — Product joining pattern design
Decision: choose attachment locations/relations between specified mating components under structural performance and join-spacing limits. Artifact: weld/fastener/bond pattern in the product definition. Aliases: spot-weld selection, attachment topology, weld-pattern optimization. Direct evidence is strongest for discrete weld sites; other joint mechanisms are variants requiring their own physical evaluation.
DIRECT S09. IE: joining engineering and multidisciplinary structural evaluation. OR: discrete joining-topology optimization or surrogate-assisted multidiscipline search. Industrial manual review and numerical design evaluation are distinct from mathematical optimality claims.
Primary #design; Primary #link. Each retained attachment establishes a physical relation between components. Choosing a subset of joint sites is the representation of this linking contract and does not require redundant #select. Joint coordinates need #place only when continuously repositioned, not merely because fixed candidate sites have coordinates.
Distinct from LOADPATH's structural member network and MATERIAL's grade/gauge assignments. Robot weld sequence and manufacturing station capacity remain AUTOM. Product join feasibility is an interface input to process engineering; it does not absorb assembly line design.
Ratings INFERENCE: Priority High; Relevance High; Hardness High. Automotive joining patterns recur; structural load cases and permitted attachment patterns require tailored evaluation.
LOADPATH, MATERIAL, PACK → JOIN → TOL, VALIDATE. New tolerance requirements may require join-layout revision; joint optimization is recognized.

TOL — Discrete tolerance assignment
Decision: assign allowed tolerance classes to specified product dimensions/features under assembly-function and achievable-capability requirements. Artifact: tolerance values/classes on the released design. Aliases: tolerance allocation, tolerance-cost optimization, statistical tolerancing. Variants: body gaps/flushness, drivetrain stacks and component families.
DIRECT S10; qualification S11. IE: tolerance-stack and statistical variation analysis, including Monte Carlo; capability/cost-based design. OR: discrete tolerance-cost optimization with variation simulation or surrogate evaluation; evolutionary search when responses are nonlinear. Conventional engineering trial-and-error remains an industrial baseline; a research algorithm is not assumed universal deployed practice.
Primary #design; Primary #map. Assign finite classes to dimensions. The word allocation does not make the Regime #allocate: the choice defines the product rather than distributes installed scarce capacity. Continuous-only tolerance optimization is excluded from this card.
Different from AUTOM's selective component matching, which pairs manufactured instances with measured deviations. Process alternatives that set manufacturing capability belong AUTOM when the artifact is a manufacturing route; joint product/process models preserve that interface.
Ratings INFERENCE: Priority High; Relevance High; Hardness High. Tolerance commitments affect quality and manufacturing cost; nonlinear stacks and variation evaluation constrain choices. Easier separable/smooth restrictions do not negate industrial coupling.
CONFIG, MATERIAL, PACK, JOIN → TOL → VALIDATE. Dimensional feedback can require JOIN or CONFIG revision.

PROTOTYPE — Prototype fleet configuration
Decision: choose how many validation vehicles/specimens to create and configure their finite options so the selected validation demands have compatible buildable specimens. Artifact: prototype fleet definitions, not test start times. Aliases: test-vehicle configuration, pre-production vehicle configuration, prototype variant selection. Variants: coverage of option interactions, minimum fleet size, feasible reuse across tests and jointly scheduled prototypes.
DIRECT S12; joint planning S14/S15. IE: prototype-buildability and test-coverage planning. OR: SAT/Max-SAT or MILP-compatible configuration and coverage optimization; greedy coverage baseline; decomposition for integrated scheduling. The quantum formulation is an alternative encoding/method, not a new Problem or established superior Template.
Primary #design; Primary #select with Secondary #map. Select participating prototype slots and map allowed feature choices to them. It creates purpose-built development assets before their named tests are scheduled, rather than allocating an existing fleet to tests. TESTCAL performs that downstream assignment.
Given test requirements do not imply that the validation tests are selected here; VALIDATE supplies the chosen program where it is an optimized decision. Build quantities and vehicle definitions constrain calendar feasibility. Prototype fabrication start times are not independently admitted from S12's misleading production title.
Ratings INFERENCE: Priority High; Relevance High; Hardness Medium. Compatibility has many rules, but SAT/MILP and greedy coverage have directly demonstrated practical instances. Tight calendar, degradation and simultaneous configuration may raise Hardness in the integrated variant; formal SAT hardness alone is insufficient for Critical.
CONFIG, VALIDATE → PROTOTYPE → TESTCAL. Calendar feasibility can force more prototypes or changed variants, giving PROTOTYPE ↔ TESTCAL. This cross-band VALIDATE → PROTOTYPE flow must remain visible after numbering.

VALIDATE — Validation test portfolio selection
Decision: select optional validation activities/scenarios to cover specified product requirements, failure modes and critical conditions within a fixed development budget, retaining all mandatory checks. Artifact: chosen validation program. Aliases: DVP selection, test suite selection, failure-mode coverage, minimum critical scenario set. Variants: component reliability, vehicle validation, discrete scenario catalogs and risk-weighted coverage.
TRANSFER S16 for failure-mode covering; DIRECT S17 for vehicle scenario selection. IE: verification/validation and failure-mode/risk coverage planning.
