PROBLEM MARKETPLACE

Purpose
Preserve materially credible Problem candidates transferred between Domains.

Record
CANDIDATE | ORIGIN | PROPOSED OWNER | REASON | EVIDENCE | STATUS

Status
OPEN      Origin excludes the candidate but considers it materially ICO and plausibly owned elsewhere.
CLAIMED   Proposed owner admits it as a distinct Problem.
MERGED    Proposed owner absorbs it into an admitted Problem.
REJECTED  Proposed owner determines that it is not a separate Problem or not owned there.

Rules
- Origin records the transfer but does not classify another Domain's foreground.
- Proposed owner resolves the candidate during normal research and control.
- Keep the record when resolved.
- Do not use the marketplace as a substitute for evidence in the receiving Domain's research background.
