TERRITORY AND OWNERSHIP

INTEL owns decisions whose adversarial, protective, security, denial or strategic-response conditions define the industrial contract. It spans defense capability management, intelligence collection, protective security and cybersecurity. Military ownership alone is insufficient. Conversely, an enterprise security or privacy decision need not be military to belong here.
Industrial coverage: defense organizations; intelligence collection; airport, seaport and border security; safeguards verification; critical infrastructure; enterprise and operational-technology cyber defense; conservation enforcement; protective data release. These intersect ##DEFENSE, ##AEROSPACE, ##AIRLINE, ##MARITIME, ##POWER, ##TELECOM, ##FINANCE, ##HEALTHCARE and other open Industry contexts. Industry labels do not create Problems.
COMP owns civilian observation, ordinary compute/network scheduling and non-adversarial digital restoration. LOG owns pure journeys, transport and deployment movement. SCM owns provisioning and inventories. HR owns standalone staffing and training rosters. CIVIC owns civic-service coordination and routine public-asset upkeep. ENER owns utility production and service operation. LAND and EXTR own resource management and extraction; protective anti-poaching or anti-intrusion commitments can belong to INTEL. FIN owns financial positions and reconciliation; MKT owns procurement mechanisms and traded opportunities. HC owns integrated clinical response.

COVERAGE, MERGES AND EXCLUSIONS

Physical protection: hardening covers permanent asset protection; sensor covers protective detection layout; patrol covers protective duty schedules, fixed coverage and mobile escorts; alarm covers a triggered response. Anti-poaching is a patrol Industry context, not a LAND resource-harvest problem.
Verification: screening covers people/consignments crossing access boundaries; inspection covers selected visits and samples against concealed noncompliance. Travel execution within either is not automatically a separate INTEL Problem.
Intelligence: collection includes satellite observation and dynamic retasking. Search retains path-constrained information acquisition for unlocated objects. Signal classification, target tracking, data fusion and threat scoring alone are inference tasks rather than admitted resource decisions. Evidence did not justify a separate canonical exploitation-capacity Problem: generic digital queues and analyst rosters belong to COMP/HR, and integrated intelligence-priority variants remain collection interfaces pending a distinct supported contract.
Defense: capability, readiness, mission, interdiction, effect and electronic have distinct outputs. Air defense is an integrated application of collection and effect allocation, not a duplicate Problem. Wargaming is a method/context, not a stand-alone decision. Ordinary base construction, maintenance, spares, training timetables and deployment transportation remain CIVIC, LOG, SCM, HR or PROD according to contract.
Cyber: controls selects an investment portfolio; access creates authorization roles; segmentation creates security boundaries; deception deploys decoys; reconfiguration chooses recurring configuration changes; remediation selects a vulnerability backlog; alert admits investigation work; investigation chooses evidence actions; response contains and recovers a compromised system. Attack-graph construction, malware classification and intrusion scoring are evidence inputs unless separately tied to a material discrete industrial commitment. Firewall rule pruning and key-management variants are not promoted without a distinct contract beyond access/segmentation/controls.
Privacy: privacy retains group/generalization/suppression decisions. Noise calibration, cryptographic algorithm design and continuous privacy-budget allocation alone are excluded. k-anonymity is a scoped Template, not a universal privacy guarantee.
Cross-domain and low-evidence candidates: standalone financial fraud adjudication, generic audit sampling, political influence allocation, offensive exploit construction and abstract pursuit/evasion games are not silently included by keyword. A future distinct candidate requires its own industrial contract and Template evidence. Integrated cyber-physical protection can use ENER operating models while remaining INTEL; routine reliability optimization remains with the asset domain.
No permanent Cases, benchmark IDs, data-access claims, reference-result tables or benchmark selections are made. Later Case work requires human approval of the foreground Problem set.

investigation → response | Evidence refines incident state and appropriate countermeasures. S33/S34; synthesis.

The patrol/alarm, collection/search and investigation/response loops are explicit feedback. Initial schedules or tasking precede event-conditioned updates. No optimization-derived edge is inferred solely because two Problems share a formulation.
