DOMAIN TERRITORY

CIVIC covers shared civic assets, public-service coordination, public works and construction execution. A candidate must contain a material discrete construction or choice. Public ownership, a map, a network model or a safety requirement alone is insufficient.
Industry discovery spans ##CONSTRUCTION, ##EDUCATION, ##WATER, transport infrastructure, parking, municipal waste, public facilities and urban runoff control. Industry labels are open cross-classification terms, not ownership rules. ##MUNICIPAL, ##PARKING and ##TRANSPORTINFRA are useful proposed industry refinements, not new closed Domains.

Operating systems: public capital programs; public facility and school networks; shared roads and parking sites; civil load-bearing structures; local runoff-control retrofits; municipal service territories and recurring calendars; civic asset inspection and renewal; road and rail works access; multi-utility excavation coordination; temporary site facilities; project and work-front scheduling; workspace and lifting coordination; live street permissions; physical asset and construction schedule recovery.

Boundary rule: distinguish the delivered contract from adjacent system models. Shared road capacity and signal permissions are CIVIC. Carrier routes, transit line plans, vehicle circulation, railway train paths and transport recovery are LOG. The transport models can evaluate or consume CIVIC commitments without changing their ownership. Generic crews and rosters remain HR; construction activity calendars with supplied crew capacity remain CIVIC. ENER owns utility production, balance, switching and service operating recovery; CIVIC owns service-asset physical repair and shared work-access calendars. LAND owns sites, land-use designations, catchments, basin allocations and water rights. Urban built runoff-control installation choices remain CIVIC in the narrowly specified contract. COMP owns sensing and digital resources, not the civic task that consumes their observations. MKT owns pricing, rights and allocation mechanisms; FIN owns financing instruments and positions. HC owns clinical/direct-care feasibility; INTEL owns adversarial or protective response conditions that dominate the decision.

EVIDENCE METHOD AND LIMITS

Material problem and Template claims use source handles S01-S71 below. Original papers, official agency tools/guidance and author-institution records were used. Some publisher pages expose only abstracts or search-index excerpts. A cited method is established enough to support a Problem Template; it is not asserted to dominate every industrial instance. Published methods, official engineering practice and benchmark discovery are distinguished. No paper results were reproduced, no software was run and no Case was selected. Reported code/data availability is a discovery lead, not an independently checked reproducibility finding.
Candidate scope, Primary Domain, Regime, Structure, ratings and dependency directions are project synthesis under the normative ontology. They are explicitly inference unless the evidence sentence says otherwise. Evidence supports method existence and decision identity; it does not empirically calibrate the ontology's subjective ratings. Hardness judgments use coupling, scale and response requirements. Formal complexity is included only for a supported scoped result; no placeholder class statements are emitted.

Search rounds used: (1) road network design, public facilities, municipal districting and construction scheduling; (2) work zones, phase plans, adaptive signals, curb and parking allocation; (3) railway works possessions, lane reversals and school assignment; (4) discrete structural design, portfolio selection, time-cost modes and repetitive work; (5) inspection, street-service calendars and infrastructure/project recovery; (6) workspace, crane coordination and urban runoff tools; (7) official scheduling benchmark/model sources and cross-asset intervention coordination. Searches through 2026-10-07 included recent work but establish defensible approaches, not a universal SOTA ranking.

IDENTITY, MERGES AND EXCLUSIONS

Public-service facilities include civic collection/transfer nodes. Municipal waste facility siting is a retained service-specific variant, not another generic location card. Waste treatment technology/process synthesis is PROC. Waste routes and arc-service tours remain LOG15/LOG16. Pure collection routing is not promoted because the customer is a municipality.
Transit line planning, passenger timetabling, transfer synchronization, vehicle blocking, headway regulation and journey recovery remain LOG06-LOG09, LOG44 and LOG45. CIVIC represents the fixed asset and public-works interfaces. Railway movement path and platform allocation remain LOG41, even when an infrastructure manager owns the task. Airline airside allocations remain LOG; airport landside curb functions and parking capacity use the curb/parking decisions.
Standalone construction workforce sizing, rostering, crew duty legality and personnel assignment belong to HR. Construction project, repetitive work-front and workspace schedules remain CIVIC when physical construction execution defines feasibility. A simple worker-to-task assignment cannot be rescued by a construction label.
Material procurement, supplier selection and site inventory are SCM/MKT contracts; prefab production and factory assembly are PROD; process-plant operating schedules are PROC. They are inputs/interfaces to the construction cards.
Water pump/tank schedules, valve isolation, pressure recovery and installed distribution design follow ENER. Civic pipe inspection, treatment/repair programming and shared excavation calendars do not duplicate hydraulic control. Resource extraction and water withdrawal belong to EXTR. Basin allocation, watershed planning, land access and development zoning remain LAND. The runoff candidate is limited to discrete municipal built-control retrofits on supplied feasible sites.
Police, adversarial security, fire-response protective deployments and defense decisions remain INTEL when protective conditions dominate. Ambulance and clinical emergency-service decisions remain HC. Facility coverage models from those sectors are not used to infer ownership of generic civic service decisions.
Parking and school-choice auctions, revenue prices and rights mechanisms are MKT. Physical user/space assignment under given access rules remains CIVIC. Electoral districting and broad institutional governance were searched as neighbors but not admitted as commercially material civic service-asset decisions.
Construction modes and project scheduling are distinct only when the execution-mode commitment is separately delivered. A fully integrated multi-mode schedule instantiates a combined contract; it does not require two independent solver runs. Repetitive work-front scheduling retains physical location progression and continuity absent from a generic resource calendar. Workspace allocation retains explicit spatial interference; temporary facility layout retains installed site configurations; crane scheduling retains lift-to-crane choice and collision timing. Generic resource schedules must not be counted repeatedly under each alias.
Inspection acquires condition information; rehabilitation uses it to commit treatments. Work-window coordination commits shared closures; work-zone staging chooses temporary access configurations; project scheduling commits task times inside available access. Physical asset recovery chooses what to repair after failure; project schedule recovery revises an already issued construction program. These distinctions require different delivered commitments, not just different objectives.
Feasibility checking, demand estimation, condition prediction, traffic assignment and BIM conflict detection without combinatorial decisions are analyses, not admitted stand-alone Problems. Continuous optimal control, hydraulic calculations, rate-only construction planning and continuum structural optimization remain outside ICO unless the stated discrete choices are present. Algorithm names, robustness, stochasticity and multiobjective refinements are descriptors or variants, not new Problem identities.

COVERAGE ASSESSMENT AND REMAINING LIMITS

The searches covered physical design, service access, capital/treatment allocation, calendars, site operations, live permissions and disrupted commitments. Later results mainly added aliases, narrow subtypes or cross-domain interfaces. Further checks found rail possession coordination, workspace occupancy and runoff retrofit selection. Background-control searching added reusable formwork configuration and near-term asset maintenance packages; these were retained. The result is a broad v0.1 starter Problem inventory, not a claim of demonstrated exhaustive coverage.
Remaining limits: industrial Template comparisons are not uniformly available; generic MILP/CP and published heuristics are therefore retained where no universal winner is defensible. Public-school user assignment is evidence-rich but extrapolation to other civic services is an explicitly scoped family inference. Runoff retrofit ownership depends on the local built-asset contract; regional watershed or land-allocation variants require LAND. Structural design evidence is strongest for discrete trusses; other civil systems require their own response model before case admission. Formwork panel-set configuration is admitted as a distinct delivered artifact; its installation calendar remains a project/work-front variant. Short-term maintenance packages are separated from long-term treatment selection and generic crew dispatch. Small recreation facility bookings and public-counter appointments have insufficient distinct civic-asset evidence here; they are not silently elevated into standalone cards. Event/sports scheduling and educational staff/course scheduling remain HR/MKT boundary candidates until their actual decision contract is specified.
Problem Templates permit later benchmark research. PSPLIB, EPA tools, parking-layout supplemental material and published transport/crane implementations are discovery leads only. Case identity, industrial fidelity, licenses, runnable baselines and reference results remain unresearched at this layer. No Case IDs or Case cards are present.
