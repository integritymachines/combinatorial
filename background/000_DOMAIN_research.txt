SCOPED BOUNDARY RESOLUTIONS

Industry does not determine Primary Domain. Ownership follows the decision contract.

Examples:
##SEMICONDUCTOR:
->  #PROD fab scheduling, physical design, and fabrication configuration
->  #PROC separately scoped process-chemistry decisions
->  #COMP installed digital-resource operation

##REALESTATE:
->  #LAND site development, physical-space allocation, and tenant-to-space allocation
->  #MKT commercial offer, assortment, or pricing decisions
->  #FIN financing and financial-position decisions

##AIRLINE:
->  #LOG flight, fleet, journey, and disruption operations
->  #HR standalone crew and workforce systems
->  #MKT seat, offer, and revenue allocation
->  #FIN treasury and financial operations

##TELECOM:
->  #COMP compute, radio, channel, spectrum-use, and traffic-resource operation
->  #MKT rights or spectrum-auction mechanisms
->  #CIVIC physical civic infrastructure where that asset decision dominates
->  #ENER energy-supply operation where that utility decision dominates

##WATER:
->  #LAND basin, catchment, rights, and resource allocation
->  #EXTR prospecting, withdrawal, extraction, and depletion
->  #ENER installed utility pump, tank, valve, and distribution operation
->  #CIVIC service-asset maintenance, construction, and civic-service coordination

##POWERMARKETS:
->  #ENER physical commitment, dispatch, balance, and grid-feasible clearing
->  #MKT market mechanism, bid acceptance, and rights allocation
->  #FIN settlement, exposure, and financial-claim decisions

##HUMANSERVICES:
->  #HR standalone workforce, roster, or crew decisions
->  #HC integrated decisions where clinical, patient, or direct-care conditions define the promised service
->  #LOG generic transport or movement when clinical service conditions do not define the decision

##CIVICMOVEMENT:
->  #LOG pure journey, route, or movement execution
->  #CIVIC shared civic-service coordination, public works, or civic-asset operation
