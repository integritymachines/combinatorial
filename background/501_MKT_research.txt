TERRITORY AND ADMISSION

MKT owns decisions that construct commercial offers, choose bids or commercial opportunities, allocate traded rights, match counterparties, or determine commercial exposure. The exchange contract determines ownership. Monetary value is often an objective in other Domains and does not make their physical or human decisions MKT. Commercial optimization is not identical to all marketing analytics. Forecasts, elasticities, propensity models, valuations and bidding recommendations can supply inputs without creating a discrete decision.

Principal operating systems:
- Retail and e-commerce: ranges, bundles, price ladders, physical merchandising, promotion calendars, terminal clearance, targeting and ordered digital displays.
- Airline, hospitality, rental and reservation services: product/fare definitions, commercial capacity protection, oversale permissions and dynamic offer/acceptance.
- Advertising publishers: guaranteed inventory reservation, spot market allocation, break/airtime placement and delivery repair.
- Advertisers and agencies: media purchases, targeted campaign offers and budget-limited auction participation.
- Procurement, rights and service markets: bidder packages, package winner selection and contractual counterparty matching.
- Telecommunications, cloud services and spectrum: offer menus and capacity-right awards; installed resource operation remains elsewhere.
- Commercial real estate: offers and lease terms. Physical tenant-to-space allocation remains LAND.
- Multi-party collaborative systems: coalition candidates examined separately; physical collaboration evidence does not prove a stand-alone generic MKT Problem.

The candidate set must produce an industrially real subset, assignment, relation or placement. Numeric discretization solely for algorithm convenience does not change ownership or establish a stand-alone ICO contract. Continuous quantities and prices can accompany discrete menus, acceptance decisions or integer booking limits. Templates establish defensible architecture rather than a universal solver ranking. Source claims below are summarized narrowly; ontology, graph and rating conclusions are analytical inferences unless explicitly described as a source result.

OWNERSHIP AND INTERFACES

MKT / SCM: assortment and customer offers differ from stocking, replenishment and supplier supply-position choice. Auction-mechanism award is MKT; a sourcing model with quoted prices as input is SCM. A price-dependent replenishment model is owned by its dominant supply commitment, not its objective.
MKT / LOG: selling seats, service rights or delivery windows is MKT. Flights, vehicle assignments, routes and delivery execution are LOG. Bid cost envelopes may be computed by LOG without moving package submission there.
MKT / ENER: mechanism-based bid acceptance or tradable capacity-right allocation is MKT. Physical unit commitment, balance and grid-feasible clearing remain ENER, even when organized as an auction. Integrated contracts follow the dominant feasibility decision.
MKT / FIN: price offers, bidding and counterparty market allocation are MKT. Portfolio positions, risk exposure, financing, settlement and reconciliation are FIN. Generic order-book processing is not automatically a discrete MKT Problem.
MKT / COMP: commercial impressions and ranking opportunities are MKT. Compute, memory, network traffic, digital storage and operational spectrum/channel assignment are COMP. Prediction and search infrastructure may supply ranking values.
MKT / HR and HC: labor-market terms and general counterparty preferences can be commercial. Workforce assignments, skills/rostering or patient-defined allocation are owned by HR or HC. Medical matching publications are method evidence, not MKT case evidence.
MKT / LAND: physical tenant layout, parcel use and geographically constitutive territories belong to LAND. Commercial offer selection or pricing with physical feasibility supplied can be MKT.
MKT / PROC and PROD: supplied cost/feasibility envelopes constrain offers. Joint production schedules, process recipes and physical product configurations remain in their physical Domain.

RESEARCH METHOD AND COVERAGE

Discovery used the foundational MKT seeds, then searched separately for retail, revenue management, publisher/advertiser operations, procurement/rights, two-sided markets, finite pricing, physical merchandising, live control and repair. Search families included winner determination and generalized auctions; mixed-logit assortment; bundles; discrete price ladders; shelf allocation; promotions and markdowns; targeted offers; reach/frequency media buying; network booking control; no-shows; guaranteed/spot reservations; bid generation; stable matching; position effects; online matching and budget throttling; TV scheduling; sales territories; commercial coalition formation.

Source access was mixed: full text, author/institutional abstracts, publisher abstracts and primary-source indexed excerpts. Direct publisher opens that failed are identified in the register. Those sources support only the facts stated in their available abstracts; full experimental details, software access and benchmark reproducibility remain unverified. This is Problem-level research. No measured SOTA ranking, performance baseline or benchmark completeness is asserted.

Two follow-up discovery sweeps focused on omitted actors and decisions. The bidder-side participation contract added THROTTLE beyond publisher SERVE; bidder package construction remains distinct from market CLEAR. Buyer media commitments and seller airtime placements differ. General mechanism design and coalition generation require caution: not every economic model creates a supported industrial ICO Problem. Later searches mainly produced alternative formulations, narrow variants, integrated neighboring-domain contracts or candidates listed below. This is a broad v0.1 discovery result, not proof that future industrial evidence cannot add a material family.

ASSORT — Retail Assortment Selection
Decision contract: Selects products to offer by store or channel under category, range-size and supplied availability limits while accounting for substitution..
Identity and evidence: Alias: assortment optimization, product-range optimization. Variants: store clusters, channel-specific ranges, cardinality, budgets, substitution, robust demand. Do not split each choice model into a Problem. Inputs: candidate products, margins, availability envelopes and estimated preferences. Delivered artifact: range membership, not replenishment quantities. Joint position or price models couple to DISPLAY or PRICE without erasing standalone range selection. Priority reflects defining retail role; value reflects multi-product margin. S03 establishes constrained formulations and model-dependent computational difficulty; unconstrained single-segment MNL must not inherit the general difficulty. No claim that a conic architecture universally dominates. B follow-up evidence S37 explicitly separates general capacitated and mixed-MNL hardness from classical unconstrained single-MNL cases.
Candidate ontology: Primary Domain MKT. Regime #plan | Commits the commercial range before display, campaigns and live offers. Structure #select | Chooses the product subset offered in each commercial context.
Template evidence: IE / OR Category and range planning | Choice-based MILP or mixed-integer conic optimization; specialized algorithms for tractable choice models. Sources S03, S37. Source-reported methods establish architecture; rating and ownership conclusions use the contract above.
Ratings with rationale: Priority Critical | Product-range selection defines major retail and digital-commerce systems; Relevance Critical | The offered range affects sales, margin and customer substitution across large portfolios; industrial Hardness High | NP-hard for general constrained mixed-logit variants; Choice heterogeneity and category restrictions couple product selections.

Decision contract: Assigns arriving impressions or advertising slots to eligible campaigns or spot buyers under budgets, delivery obligations and exposure limits..
Identity and evidence: Aliases: ad allocation, impression assignment, advertiser-query matching. Distinct from RESERVE forecast commitments, MEDIA buyer planning and THROTTLE bidder participation. Inputs: supplied impression/campaign eligibility, bids, residual budgets and guaranteed commitments. Artifact: realized campaign/buyer assignment. Multiple-slot ranking or creative selection is an integrated variant and may need PLACE/SELECT when actually constructed; the base card does not infer these from web coordinates. Primal-dual online matching gives a benchmark architecture with competitive guarantees only under stated arrival/bid assumptions; production delivery controls use pacing/opportunity-cost heuristics. S23 supports operation but does not expose source code, data or a universal optimality guarantee. Hardness refers to online uncertainty and practical constraints, not an invented NP-hardness theorem.
Candidate ontology: Primary Domain MKT. Regime #dispatch | Commits a campaign or buyer for each current commercial opportunity. Structure #map | Assigns current impressions or slots to campaigns or buyers.
Template evidence: IE / OR Publisher ad serving and guaranteed-versus-spot yield control | Online budgeted matching, primal-dual allocation and delivery-aware opportunity-cost controls. Sources S22, S23. Source-reported methods establish architecture; rating and ownership conclusions use the contract above.
Ratings with rationale: Priority Critical | Serving allocation defines online advertising markets; Relevance Critical | Decisions affect publisher yield and advertiser contract delivery at large scale; industrial Hardness High | Online budgeted matching; Unknown future arrivals, delivery coupling and latency make policy quality difficult despite simple local assignments.
Useful refinements: ##ADVERTISING ##online-matching ##guaranteed-delivery. Compute-resource scheduling belongs to COMP; commercial impression assignment belongs to MKT.

THROTTLE — Budget-Constrained Auction Participation
Decision contract: Selects which arriving advertising auctions to enter under remaining spend, expected value and campaign timing conditions..
Identity and evidence: Aliases: auction throttling, hard pacing. Distinguish an advertiser's entry decision from publisher SERVE. A supplied recommended bid/valuation is input; the selected auction subset is a discrete artifact. Probabilistic implementation delivers entry/no-entry decisions but should not imply that merely tuning one probability without an optimized allocation policy is a separate ICO Problem. Soft pacing may participate in every auction with scaled bids; that continuous control is excluded unless accompanied by an independent finite bid/entry choice. Source directly compares throttling with pacing and qualifies stochastic/adversarial performance. No universal advantage claimed for either. Offline budgeted auction selection supplies a diagnostic bound or benchmark, not an implementable clairvoyant live policy. Adaptation consumes observed spend and value; it is not a distinct generic pacing Problem.
Candidate ontology: Primary Domain MKT. Regime #dispatch + #adapt | Commits auction entry and revises participation as spend and opportunities change. Structure #select | Chooses the auctions in which the advertiser participates.
Template evidence: IE / OR Advertiser budget throttling and participation control | Online threshold or gradient-based participation policies; stochastic budgeted selection and offline IP baselines. Sources S24. Source-reported methods establish architecture; rating and ownership conclusions use the contract above.
Ratings with rationale: Priority High | Discrete participation control recurs in budget-limited advertising buying; Relevance High | Participation affects campaign return and budget use; industrial Hardness High | Online budgeted selection; Future auction values and prices are uncertain, and performance guarantees depend on their arrival model.
Useful refinements: ##ADVERTISING ##throttling ##budget-control ##online. Continuous bid-multiplier tuning alone is outside this participation-selection scope.

AIRTIME — Advertising Break and Airtime Scheduling
Decision contract: Places accepted commercials in broadcast periods, breaks and ordered positions under durations, audience commitments, separation and rotation rules..
Identity and evidence: Aliases: commercial scheduling, pod assignment, broadcast traffic scheduling. Distinguish fixed/bought campaign commitments from MEDIA purchases and RESERVE capacity reservations. Variants: competitor exclusion, first/last rotation, uncertain viewers, multiple stations, make-goods and rolling rescheduling. RESOLVED ADMISSION AND DIRECTION

PRICE requires actual permitted commercial price levels. A solver-only grid does not qualify. CAPACITY and RESERVE require discrete commitments, not only continuous LP outputs. OFFER supplies currently available alternatives to DISPLAY. Observed display response is an estimation input and does not justify a direct DISPLAY -> OFFER Problem edge. THROTTLE precedes CLEAR within a repeated auction, so Regime labels must not override the input/output order. Territorial, coalition and mechanism candidates remain deferred or neighboring-domain scopes under the reasons below. Knapsack and generalized-assignment hardness is qualified to the restriction; online difficulty remains distinct from offline computational complexity.

ALIASES, VARIANTS, EXCLUSIONS AND DEFERRED CANDIDATES

Merge:
- Forward, reverse, multi-unit and exchange winner determination under CLEAR; each selects accepted bid commitments. Do not add a Problem per auction direction or solver.
- Coupon, retention, personalized-discount and direct-marketing assignments under TARGET when customer-offer allocation is the artifact.
- Fare-class and room-class protection under CAPACITY; industry or resource incidence does not create a second family.
- Hard pacing under THROTTLE; publisher delivery pacing is a control component of SERVE or RESERVE.
- Broadcast competitor separation, pod assignment, rotation and make-good placement under AIRTIME.
- Dynamic contract acceptance and offer availability under OFFER when both are finite live selling actions over supplied capacity.
- Choice-model, robust/stochastic and solver-specific changes remain variants unless they change the industrial artifact.

Keep distinct:
ASSORT membership / BUNDLE composition / PRICE level assignment / SHELF physical commercial layout / DISPLAY ordered digital exposure.
PROMO repeated event calendar / MARKDOWN terminal residual-stock trajectory.
CAPACITY commercial protection policy / OVERBOOK authorized oversale risk.
MEDIA advertiser purchases / RESERVE publisher forecast commitments / SERVE current impression assignments / AIRTIME detailed future/current airing placements.
BID bidder contents / THROTTLE bidder live participation / CLEAR market winning packages / MATCH preference or welfare counterpart assignments.

Exclude as stand-alone MKT Problems:
- Demand forecasting, elasticity estimation, propensity scoring, valuation, click prediction and causal response estimation: analytical inputs, no admitted combinatorial allocation.
- Pure continuous pricing, bid multiplier tuning, pacing probability optimization, monetary budget splitting or continuous risk valuation without a material discrete output.
- Applying an already supplied price, threshold, auction rule or ranking score deterministically, without optimizing competing finite alternatives.
- Pure sales-force rostering and representative-to-customer workload assignment: HR where human capacity dominates.
- Warehouse slotting: LOG; supply replenishment and shortage allocation: SCM; shelf merchandising is SHELF.
- Aircraft displacement/reaccommodation or transport capacity construction: LOG; clinical no-show/appointment policies: HC where direct care defines feasibility.
- Portfolio trade quantities, investment selection and financial settlement: FIN. Auction architecture in a financial industry does not override a financially constituted position contract.
- Station repacking/channels and installed radio-resource use: COMP; rights winners: CLEAR.
- Power market clearing with physical commitment/grid balance: ENER; separate offered rights or bid-mechanism acceptance: CLEAR.
- Physical tenant mix, facility siting and geographic land layout: LAND. Merely maximizing rent does not transfer ownership.
- Integrated order acceptance and production scheduling: PROD/PROC where transformation feasibility is constructed, illustrated by S28.

Deferred, with reasons:
- Auction-rule/mechanism optimization: S29 supports a complex mechanism design project; it does not establish one commercially recurring finite rule-selection contract and benchmark Template distinct from clearing. Fixed mechanism is input here. Reopen with evidence of actual optimized finite rule architecture.
- Sales territory/account portfolio design: S31 establishes geographic territory models. Geography, worker capacity and commercial opportunity can lead to different Primary Domains. No generic MKT family is admitted from the shared name. Reopen a commercial-only account/right allocation contract with geography and staffing supplied.
- Commercial coalition/consortium formation: S32 establishes generic set partitioning; S33 establishes integrated physical joint ordering. Generic commercial alliance feasibility, value data and operational ownership remain insufficiently resolved to add a canonical MKT Problem. Integrated physical collaboration remains SCM/LOG; no universal MKT coalition claim.
- Loyalty program tier/rule design, commercial channel-network design and finite mechanism parameter portfolios: potentially discrete, but no distinct strongly supported IE/OR contract after this discovery. Do not force Templates to gain coverage.
- Standalone commercial contract rescue or generic repair: revisions belong to the existing acceptance, reservation, matching or scheduling family; separate admission requires a different delivered artifact.
- Barter exchanges, spectrum/airport/cloud rights and service-contract auctions: CLEAR or MATCH variants when the commercial award dominates; physical execution remains with the owning Domain.

LIMITS AND LATER CASE RESEARCH

The problem set covers established finite commercial decisions; it does not imply that every marketing optimization or every marketplace is combinatorial. Constrained variants may be hard while classical assignment, flow or basic choice models are tractable. Rating judgments use the stated scope, not only formal complexity. Direct restriction arguments are marked explicitly in the relevant dossiers. No unattested full-contract complexity labels are filled in for visual uniformity.

Cases need separate human approval. Later work must test the Template against current implementations and public reproducibility, identify meaningful industrial instances, record license/access conditions, and compare real baselines. CATS-style auction generators, published assortment instances and operational media records are discovery leads, not selected or numbered Cases. Historical deployment statements remain historical; none is asserted to still be deployed without verification.
