WATER — Water Utility Operations
v0.1 | 10 October 2026 | Case layer

SCOPE
Macro Domain anchor: #INFRA. Primary Domain: 301 #ENER. Industry: ##WATER. Territory: #PRODUCTION.
WATER covers installed water-service capability, pumping, storage, hydraulic districting, pressure control, network disinfectant capability, service scheduling and hydraulic or quality recovery. It includes wastewater-transfer pumping when equipment actions and hydraulic service define the contract.
Ownership follows the installed utility-service commitment. Basin rights, extraction, treatment-process transformation, civic asset maintenance, construction, standalone sensing, workforce and vehicle journeys remain neighboring contracts.

WATER101 Water Network Configuration and Pipe Sizing.
DESCRIPTION Chooses permitted pipe connections and assigns catalogue sizes to pipe reaches under demand, pressure and investment limits.
TEMPLATE Hydraulic network capacity design | Mixed-integer convex design for supported resistance models; MINLP or hydraulic simulation with evolutionary search for extensions.
REGIME #design | Commits pipe capacity and connections before operating plans.
STRUCTURE #map + #link | Assigns pipe sizes to reaches and constructs permitted new connections.
PRIORITY Critical | Pipe capacity and connectivity define the core service envelope.
RELEVANCE Critical | Network choices commit long-lived capacity and capital.
HARDNESS High | Discrete pipe options interact with nonconvex hydraulic feasibility.
DEPENDENCIES ∅ → WATER101 → WATER102, WATER103, WATER104, WATER105, WATER106, WATER302.
TAGS #INFRA #ENER ##WATER #PRODUCTION #design #map #link #strategic ##network-design ##pipe-sizing.
COMMENTS Fixed-topology sizing omits connection choices. Condition-based renewal portfolios and construction work belong to civic asset management.

WATER302c Pump and Valve Scheduling Benchmark.
CASE Schedule installed pump starts, stops, speeds and material valve states over a forecast horizon while maintaining storage and hydraulic service constraints.
STAGE Researched | INDUSTRY.
SOTA Calibrated hydraulic digital twin with current operating state and short-horizon demand, source and energy forecasts; mathematical or simulation-based optimization produces an equipment schedule that is checked by hydraulic look-ahead simulation.
CONTEXT Drinking-water production, transmission and distribution | Multiple pump stations, tanks and controllable valves; fixed/VFD equipment, nonlinear hydraulics, time-varying energy conditions, switching restrictions and terminal storage requirements.
GAP Public benchmarks are established but largely based on older simplified EPANET networks and do not jointly represent current digital-twin state initialization, mixed fixed/VFD equipment, material valve actions, forecast uncertainty, dynamic energy signals, switch wear and multi-objective safety conditions | Proposed uplift direction: test v10 structural decomposition or move design on a purpose-built multi-regime hydraulic benchmark against strong hierarchical, MILP/relaxation, predictive-control and simulation-optimization baselines.
ATTEMPTS Current methods include hierarchical digital-twin scheduling, nonlinear or mixed-integer optimization, hydraulic relaxations with validation, simulation-based metaheuristics and predictive control; reported gains are mostly against conventional operation or local rules rather than common SOTA baselines.
TAGS #INFRA #ENER ##WATER #PRODUCTION #plan #place #select #rolling ##pump-scheduling ##digital-twin ##benchmark.
COMMENTS Day-ahead or issued equipment calendars belong here; immediate state-feedback pump and valve actions remain WATER801.
