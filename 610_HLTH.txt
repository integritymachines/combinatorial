HLTH — Healthcare
v0.1 | 9 October 2026 | Problem layer

SCOPE
Macro Domain anchor: #HUMAN. Primary Domain: 601 #HC. Industry: ##HEALTHCARE. Territory: #PRODUCTION.
HLTH covers direct healthcare services whose clinical, patient or care conditions define feasible decisions. It spans clinical service and response-system configuration, hospital and ambulatory care, patient-specific radiation delivery, transplantation, transfusion, home care and public-health treatment access.
Ownership follows the delivered clinical service or patient-care commitment. Standalone workforce duties, supply and replenishment, generic transport, billing, construction, manufacturing and digital-resource operation remain with their corresponding Domains. Clinical trials that develop new efficacy evidence are outside this service-production territory.
