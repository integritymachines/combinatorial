COMP Computational and Digital Infrastructure
v0.1 | Problem layer | 2026-10-07
Normative: 000_DOMAIN.txt v0.4. Evidence: background/401_COMP_research.txt v0.1.

COMP covers discrete decisions that configure and operate computation, communication, storage and civilian sensing. The set spans digital capacity, executable program plans, resource commitments, live execution and service-preserving change. Industry does not determine ownership: semiconductor physical design belongs to PROD; utility supply to ENER; commercial rights to MKT; adversarial security and ISR to INTEL.

Dependencies are conditional industrial decision flow. The sequence follows first-pass commitments and retains five feedback returns. A host assignment is #map; a time or spectrum interval is #place. Pure continuous control is outside these cards unless it forms part of a discrete decision.

Templates identify defensible established approaches, not a universal performance ranking. Ratings apply to each stated scope. Cases await human approval.
