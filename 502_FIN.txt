FIN Finance, Accounting and Insurance
v0.1 | Problem layer | 2026-10-07
Normative: 000_DOMAIN.txt v0.4. Evidence: background/502_FIN_research.txt v0.1.

FIN covers discrete decisions over financial positions, claims, liquidity, settlement, accounting matches and insurance-risk rules. The set spans financial commitments, position allocation, execution, post-trade operations, accounting assurance and financial rescue. A financial industry does not determine ownership: commercial offers and auction winners belong to MKT; physical supply decisions to SCM; physical cash movement to LOG; workforce schedules to HR; adversarial investigations to INTEL; ledger infrastructure to COMP.

Each card requires a material discrete artifact. Continuous valuation, forecasting, risk estimation and deterministic administration supply inputs. Continuous quantities may accompany discrete decisions. Templates identify defensible established architectures; they do not assert a universal performance ranking or public reproducibility of private systems.

Dependencies are conditional industrial or information flow. The sequence places first-pass commitments before their consumers and retains five returns to a later iteration. Independent financial systems need not implement every edge. Integrated models can co-decide connected Problems. Ratings apply to the stated scope. Cases await human approval.
