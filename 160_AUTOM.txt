AUTOM — Automotive Manufacturing
v0.1 | 10 October 2026 | Case layer

SCOPE
Industry: ##AUTOMOTIVE. Primary Domain: 102 #PROD. Territory: #production. Macro Domain anchor: #TRANS.
Manufacturing capability, fabrication, physical assembly and recovery of released vehicle and component designs define this Domain. Product-platform and family-architecture decisions belong to a separate automotive development territory. Factory capability design remains production.
Supply commitments, independent movement, workforce arrangements, utility operation, chemical recipes and software operation remain with SCM, LOG, HR, ENER, PROC and COMP when those contracts define the decision.
